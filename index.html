I research how computational systems represent, learn, and evaluate architectural design knowledge. Shape grammar is the formal ground of that work, connected to AI, CAD data, and building performance simulation. I teach the same thing I study: how to make design knowledge explicit enough to compute with, and clear enough to argue with.
