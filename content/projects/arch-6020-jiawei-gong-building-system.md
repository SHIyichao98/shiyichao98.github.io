## Course

Media + Modeling II teaches computational methods for architectural representation, modeling, simulation and fabrication at the graduate level. Course Coordinator Hyojin Kwon led Section A; I was the instructor of Section B. This work was produced in my section, under my direct supervision.

## Project

Drawing on the Melbourne School of Design and the A Coruna Arts Centre, the scheme separates a shell structure from a beam system inside it. Frosted glass veils the interior, with clear openings that reveal the stair, bridges and coloured volumes behind. Plan, elevation and section perspective were followed by a 3D-printed model of the whole cube.
