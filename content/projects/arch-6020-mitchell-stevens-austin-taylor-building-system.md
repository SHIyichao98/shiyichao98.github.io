## Course

Media + Modeling II teaches computational methods for architectural representation, modeling, simulation and fabrication at the graduate level. Course Coordinator Hyojin Kwon led Section A; I was the instructor of Section B. This work was produced in my section, under my direct supervision.

## Project

The deep, scattered openings of Le Corbusier's chapel at Ronchamp are studied in plan and elevation, then rewritten with Shape Machine and Grasshopper as a structural grid with a diagonal infill of light-catching panels. Renderings test the interior light; a 3D-printed model shows the layered facade.
