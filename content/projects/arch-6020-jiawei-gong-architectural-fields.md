## Course

Media + Modeling II teaches computational methods for architectural representation, modeling, simulation and fabrication at the graduate level. Course Coordinator Hyojin Kwon led Section A; I was the instructor of Section B. This work was produced in my section, under my direct supervision.

## Project

Basic elements, a grid and a set of attractors are combined in Grasshopper: the distance to each attractor sets the radius of an arc and the rotation of a square, and the fields overlap into one drawing. The result reads as a landscape of pattern, with denser and quieter regions where the fields meet.
