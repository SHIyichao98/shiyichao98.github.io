## Overview

ARCH 6020: Media + Modeling II introduces computational methods for architectural representation, modeling, simulation, and fabrication. Through lectures, technical workshops, and design exercises, students develop algorithmic approaches to design while critically examining the capacities and limitations of computational tools.

The course connects digital representation with architectural form and material systems through a semester-long sequence of projects, moving from parametric drawing and geometric operations to rule-based design, simulation, and visualization.

## Focus

- Shape Grammar & Rule-Based Design
- Parametric Modeling with Rhino + Grasshopper
- Algorithmic Design & Computational Geometry
- 2D Parti to 3D Massing
- Patterning, Simulation & Building Systems
