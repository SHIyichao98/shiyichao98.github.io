## Overview

LOOPS is a modular elastic robotic structure designed to self-form, change shape, and move through robotically controlled material deformation. The system aggregates bending-active tensile hybrid (BATH) modules whose global geometry changes through coordinated cable actuation and elastic behavior.

The research combines parametric design, calibrated physical simulation, robotic fabrication, and real-time control. Rhino/Grasshopper and Kangaroo were used to generate and test different topologies and actuation sequences, while physical prototypes were connected to a cyber-physical network linking simulation, robotic control, and user interaction.

Second author and lead student researcher, contributing to the design development, computational simulation, robotic prototyping, and documentation of the LOOPS research project within the BPro AD program at The Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL.
