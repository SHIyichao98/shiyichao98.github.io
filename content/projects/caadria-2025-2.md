## Overview

This research presents an interactive generative AI workflow for Traditional Chinese Garden design. The system combines DreamBooth, LoRA, and ControlNet to generate masterplans that respond to both spatial requirements and stylistic preferences. Users define proportions of key landscape components through color-coded segmentation maps, refine the layouts through an interactive interface, and generate final masterplans using text-based style controls.

The workflow connects dataset preparation, diffusion-model fine-tuning, structural conditioning, and user interaction. Traditional Chinese Garden masterplans are segmented into seven spatial categories and used to train specialized diffusion models. A web interface then allows users to control component ratios, modify generated layouts with brush tools, and produce garden designs that maintain the intended spatial organization while supporting different visual styles.
