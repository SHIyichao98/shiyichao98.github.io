## Overview

ARCH 2017: Architecture Design Studio 3 develops architectural design through the integration of building, site, program, and representation. Students work through a sequence of analytical and design exercises that connect precedent studies, drawing, digital modeling, fabrication, and image-making.

The semester progresses from case-study analysis and spatial typologies to bricolage drawings, physical and digital models, constructed images, and a final comprehensive proposal for a contemporary art gallery in Sweet Auburn. The studio treats drawing, modeling, fabrication, and AI-assisted workflows as connected methods for developing architectural ideas across multiple scales.

## Focus

- Architectural Precedent & Spatial Typology
- Bricolage, Montage & Representation
- Digital and Physical Model Making
- AI-Assisted Image & Design Workflows
- Site, Material, Circulation & Spatial Synthesis

## Role

Patrick Danahy served as Course Coordinator, with five instructors leading individual studio sections. I served as one of the Studio Instructors, responsible for guiding and evaluating my assigned section within the shared course framework.
