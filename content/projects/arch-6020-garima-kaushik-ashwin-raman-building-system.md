## Course

Media + Modeling II teaches computational methods for architectural representation, modeling, simulation and fabrication at the graduate level. Course Coordinator Hyojin Kwon led Section A; I was the instructor of Section B. This work was produced in my section, under my direct supervision.

## Project

Starting from a reconstructed section, a field of plus-shaped columns is set on a strict grid and varied three ways: cantilevered spans, sparsely spaced columns, a central atrium. A magenta facade of linear and curved elements is laid over the concrete frame, so that walking through the space turns the grid from static order into shifting rhythm. Built as a physical model.
