## Course

AI-Enhanced Design Methods for Architecture is an elective open to undergraduate and graduate students, on generative AI workflows and their integration with parametric design tools. I was the Instructor of Record, responsible for the course as a whole, and taught and evaluated this student's work directly.

## Project

Each sheet takes a single subject and asks the model to keep it: a brick gallery interior in different materials, a timber house model under different roofs, a seaside pavilion across two dozen near-identical views. The last sheet reaches for painted arcades, testing how far the same control carries into an illustrative style.
