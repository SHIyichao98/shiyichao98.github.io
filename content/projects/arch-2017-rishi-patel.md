## Course

Architecture Design Studio 3 is a second-year undergraduate studio, coordinated by Patrick Danahy with five instructors each leading a section. I led one of those sections as its studio instructor. This student was in my section: the work here was developed under my direct guidance and evaluated by me.

## Project

A folded concrete precedent is modelled first; then a series of montages tests ideas against the real street: a mural wrapped onto a facade, white volumes set into an existing lot, a brick building cut in section, and a field of seats in a yard. Each image is a quick argument rather than a finished design.
