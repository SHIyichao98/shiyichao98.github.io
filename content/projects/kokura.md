## Overview

The old town around Kokura Station is dense, low and out of reach of heavy construction equipment. The scheme adopts a 3-metre cubic module, develops a family of module types, and assembles them on site according to where people actually move: a shopping mall where the station square is busiest, terraces and split levels opening into courtyards. Because of the long rainy season, the new buildings join each other and the station through covered interior space.

## Role

Team of three, Kokura, Japan, autumn 2016. Chief designer: concept, general structure and modelling.
