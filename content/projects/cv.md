## Academic Profile

Yichao Shi is an instructor and PhD student in Architecture at the Georgia Institute of Technology. His research focuses on computational design, shape grammars, rule-based design systems, architectural AI workflows, CAD drawing analysis, and performance-aware generative design, connecting architectural computation with machine learning, simulation, visualization, and the representation of design knowledge. His teaching spans the architectural design studio, computational media and modeling, AI-enhanced design methods, parametric modeling, simulation, fabrication-oriented workflows, and critical approaches to emerging technologies in design practice.

## Research and Teaching Areas

- Computational design, shape grammars, and rule-based design systems
- AI-enhanced architectural design, generative image workflows, and design knowledge representation
- CAD drawing analysis, shape grammar inference, and machine learning for architectural data
- Performance-aware generative design, daylight simulation, and building performance workflows
- Parametric modeling, Rhino and Grasshopper, computational geometry, visualization, and digital representation

## Courses Taught

- [ARCH 8833: AI-Enhanced Design Methods for Architecture](#project/arch-8833), Spring 2025. Instructor of Record. Developed and taught an elective, open to undergraduate and graduate students, on AI-assisted design workflows: generative image methods, Stable Diffusion, ControlNet, LoRA model training, ComfyUI pipelines, Rhino and Grasshopper integration, machine-learning tools for parametric design, and the ethical use of AI in architectural practice.
- [ARCH 2017: Architecture Design Studio 3](#project/arch-2017), Spring 2026. Studio Instructor. Led and evaluated one section of the second-year undergraduate studio within a shared course framework: precedent analysis, spatial typology, drawing, digital and physical modeling, fabrication, AI-assisted image workflows, site and material thinking, and a final proposal for a contemporary art gallery in Sweet Auburn.
- [ARCH 2020: Media + Modeling 2](#project/arch-2020), Fall 2023 and Fall 2024. Co-instructor with Yeinn Oh. Taught undergraduate computational design foundations across two sections: Rhino, Grasshopper, visual scripting, algorithmic thinking, architectural geometry, attractor systems, parti development, simulation, and design variation.
- [ARCH 6020: Media + Modeling II](#project/arch-6020), Fall 2025 and Fall 2026. Instructor for Section B. Taught and evaluated a graduate computational design course: algorithmic thinking, parametric modeling, rule-based design, simulation, visualization, computational geometry, patterning, building systems, and digital design methods.
