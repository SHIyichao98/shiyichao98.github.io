## Course

AI-Enhanced Design Methods for Architecture is an elective open to undergraduate and graduate students, on generative AI workflows and their integration with parametric design tools. I was the Instructor of Record, responsible for the course as a whole, and taught and evaluated this student's work directly.

## Project

Glass pavilions, planted courtyards and a waterfront plaza are developed as a set of generated views in one consistent graphic language: pale skies, saturated colour, drawn figures. Plans and aerials are produced in the same style, so the project reads as a single scheme rather than a collection of images.
