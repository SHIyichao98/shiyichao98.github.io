## Overview

This research develops a parametric shape grammar for the computational design of traditional Chinese Dougong bracket sets based on the design principles documented in Yingzao Fashi. The framework addresses limitations of earlier computational models by introducing precise dimensional control, editable shape rules, and interactive generation within Shape Machine.

The grammar translates the modular Cai-fen measurement system and Dougong construction logic into a parameterized grid-based framework. Implemented through Shape Machine and DrawScript+, the system organizes generation into four stages: framework construction, configuration, bracket assembly, and termination. Conditional logic, loops, and editable parameters allow different bracket typologies and dimensions to be generated while maintaining traditional proportional relationships.
