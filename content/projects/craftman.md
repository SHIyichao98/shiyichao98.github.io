## Overview

Longquan celadon is losing its wood-fired kilns and the knowledge that goes with them. The workshop frames porcelain making as a spatial and cultural experience rather than a production line: spaces for making, firing, teaching, display and observation are arranged to serve the craftsman's workflow and, at the same time, a slower route for visitors through the building and the mountain landscape around it, so that the craft stays visible and can go on being taught.

## Role

Individual project: site research, concept, spatial organisation, architectural design and final presentation.
