## Overview

ARCH 2020: Media + Modeling 2 introduces parametric modeling, scripting, and digital design tools as methods for architectural design. Using Rhinoceros and Grasshopper, students learn how variables, data, geometry, and visual scripting can be used to construct and control architectural models.

The course develops both technical and conceptual skills through lectures, tutorials, workshops, and design assignments. Students move from basic algorithmic principles and parametric geometry to architectural diagramming, design variation, simulation, and systems thinking.

## Focus

- Parametric Modeling with Rhino + Grasshopper
- Visual Scripting & Algorithmic Thinking
- Architectural Geometry & Form Generation
- 2D and 3D Attractor Systems
- Parti Development, Simulation & Design Variation
