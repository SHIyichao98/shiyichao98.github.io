## ARCH 2020 · Media + Modeling 2 (2023 Fall & 2024 Fall)

### Overview

ARCH 2020: Media + Modeling 2 introduces parametric modeling, scripting, and digital design tools as methods for architectural design. Using Rhinoceros and Grasshopper, students learn how variables, data, geometry, and visual scripting can be used to construct and control architectural models.

The course develops both technical and conceptual skills through lectures, tutorials, workshops, and design assignments. Students move from basic algorithmic principles and parametric geometry to architectural diagramming, design variation, simulation, and systems thinking.

### Focus

- Parametric Modeling with Rhino + Grasshopper
- Visual Scripting & Algorithmic Thinking
- Architectural Geometry & Form Generation
- 2D and 3D Attractor Systems
- Parti Development, Simulation & Design Variation

### Role

Co-instructor with Yeinn Oh, responsible for teaching course content, technical workshops, student guidance, and evaluation across the two course sections.

## ARCH 6020 · Media + Modeling II (2025 Fall & 2026 Fall)

### Overview

ARCH 6020: Media + Modeling II introduces computational methods for architectural representation, modeling, simulation, and fabrication. Through lectures, technical workshops, and design exercises, students develop algorithmic approaches to design while critically examining the capacities and limitations of computational tools.

The course connects digital representation with architectural form and material systems through a semester-long sequence of projects, moving from parametric drawing and geometric operations to rule-based design, simulation, and visualization.

### Focus

- Shape Grammar & Rule-Based Design
- Parametric Modeling with Rhino + Grasshopper
- Algorithmic Design & Computational Geometry
- 2D Parti to 3D Massing
- Patterning, Simulation & Building Systems

### Role

Course Coordinator Hyojin Kwon led Section A, while I served as the Instructor for Section B, responsible for teaching and evaluating my assigned section.
