## Course

Media + Modeling II teaches computational methods for architectural representation, modeling, simulation and fabrication at the graduate level. Course Coordinator Hyojin Kwon led Section A; I was the instructor of Section B. This work was produced in my section, under my direct supervision.

## Project

Displacement mapping turns an image into geometry: brightness becomes height. Chase read landscape paintings by the Florida Highwaymen this way, so that sky, foliage and water become ridges and valleys, then cut the resulting field into sections and facades and 3D-printed the outcome as a dense, eroded relief.
